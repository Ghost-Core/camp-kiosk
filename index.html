Good to know

  • Flush toilets, but no showers — pack wipes and a towel for the lake.
  • Beach and canoe launch on Kiosk Lake. Canoe rentals at the Hwy 630 entrance (Algonquin North outfitters).
  • Buy firewood near the park — bringing wood from home is banned (invasive species).
  • Bear country: all food and anything scented sleeps in the car, never the tent.
  • Quiet hours from 10 pm — small campground, close neighbours.
  • Little to no cell signal — download maps and playlists before Hwy 630.
